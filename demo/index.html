      Tools

      Mouse is the shell: it sees, remembers and answers. Tools are what you switch on to make sense of what it saw. Use the ones you want.

        Plugs Coming soon

        Plugs are tools made by other people. You will be able to browse them here and add one with a click, the way you add an app to a phone.

        A few things a plug could do:

        • ReceiptsAdds up what you spent from photos of receipts.
        • Meeting notesTurns a recorded conversation into notes and to-dos.
        • RecipesSaves a photographed recipe as ingredients and steps.
        • Business cardsPulls the name, phone and email out of a card.

        None of these exist yet. They are here to show the idea.
