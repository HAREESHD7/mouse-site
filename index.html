A shell with senses, and a brain you choose

Mouse is an empty shell. It sees through a camera, listens through a microphone, speaks through a speaker, and thinks with whichever AI you connect to it. Here is how far each of those has got.

  • Sees

    Reads the words in your photos and says what each one shows: a sign, a receipt, a screen, a note.

    Works today

  • Listens

    Writes down what is said in your recordings, on your own computer, and tells it between your photos at the moment it was said. It can mishear, so the words are yours to check.

    Early

  • Speaks

    Reads a summary or an answer aloud, using a voice already on your computer.

    Early

  • Thinks

    An AI model on your own computer answers questions and writes the summary. Claude, an AI in the cloud, is a choice you make yourself, with a key of your own.

    Works today

The whole day, as text you own

Photos are hard to search and easy to forget. Mouse lays the day out in order, writes a short summary, and hands you the lot as one plain text note.

Copy it, or save it as a file. It opens in any notes app, and you can paste it into an email, a report, or a chat with any AI you like.

# Saturday in the city

2026-11-07

A Saturday in town. Coffee at Harbour Coffee
a little before nine, then the 09:32 train to

## In order

Plug in what you need

Mouse starts simple. Tools add skills, and you switch on only the ones you want.

The first tool is Nameplates, made for equipment surveys. It finds the rating plates in your photos, lays out make, model and serial number, keeps track of what you have checked, and exports the lot to Excel.

Plugs, a place to browse tools made by other people, is on the way.

Yours to keep

Your photos and notes are ordinary files in a folder on your computer. There is no account, and Mouse sends nothing anywhere on its own. Only if you connect a cloud AI yourself is what it reads sent to that AI. If Mouse disappeared tomorrow, your files would still open.

Get Mouse

Windows

Download for Windows
  1. Unzip the folder anywhere you like.
  2. Double-click Mouse.exe. Windows may say it does not know this program yet: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Mouse opens in a window of its own. To stop it, close the window.

Linux

Download for Linux
  1. Unpack the folder.
  2. Run ./mouse from inside it.
  3. Mouse opens in your web browser. Keep the terminal open while you use it.
